🎮 Game Support Knowledge Base

Quick answers for elite gamers. Get help without waiting for support.

How do I receive my game key?

Once your payment is successfully completed, your game key will be delivered instantly to your account dashboard and registered email address.

How long does delivery take?

Digital game keys are usually delivered immediately after payment confirmation. In rare cases, processing may take a few minutes.

Which payment methods are supported?

gameStoB currently supports Credit Cards, PayPal, E-Wallets, and Bank Transfers.

Can I request a refund?

Refund requests may be submitted within the eligible refund period if the purchased game key has not been activated.

What happens if my game key does not work?

Contact our support team with your order number and a screenshot of the activation error. We will investigate the issue.

Do I need an account to buy games?

Yes. An account is required to access your purchases, order history, and digital library.

Which gaming platforms are available?

gameStoB offers products for Steam, Epic Games, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, and PC gaming platforms.

Can I change my email address?

Yes. You can update your email address from your account settings after verifying ownership.
